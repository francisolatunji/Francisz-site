Care Bridge AI Project

Description: Care Bridge AI is a project that uses artificial intelligence to support caregivers and people who need help with care. The project shows how AI can be used for real-life problems by giving users a tool that connects technology with health, support, and communication.

Reflection: I am interested in this project because it shows that AI can be more than just a chatbot or school tool. It can help families, patients, and caregivers by making information easier to access and by supporting people who may need help. Building and learning about this project helped me understand how software can be used to solve meaningful problems.
